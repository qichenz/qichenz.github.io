My academic background and interests are in how emerging technologies (primarily internet technologies) shape society, and how culture and human behavior evolve alongside them.

Here's an informal "syllabus" of evergreen pieces and new topics that have informed how I think about the future.

  • Bertrand Russell, "In Praise of Idleness", 1932
  • Theodor Adorno and Max Horkheimer, "The Culture Industry", 1947
  • Donna Haraway, "A Cyborg Manifesto", 1985
  • Ursula K. Le Guin, "The Carrier Bag Theory of Fiction", 1986
  • Ethan Zuckerman, "The Internet's Original Sin", 2014
  • Emily M. Bender, Timnit Gebru, Angelina McMillan-Major and Margaret Mitchell, "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots", 2021
  • Tim Berners-Lee, "Marking the Web's 35th Birthday", 2024
