I've worked in software since 2013, both as an individual builder and a people & team leader.

A few select roles and work projects, in chronological order:

  • At BuzzFeed, I was the first internationalization product manager and launched 4 editions in Canada, Germany, Japan, and Mexico. I spent a bunch of time working with engineers to make launching new editions turnkey. I also built video editing tools there.
  • Do you listen to personalized playlists on Spotify? I worked on Daily Mixes and Discover Weekly, improving recommendations powered by Word2Vec models.
  • As a group product manager at Mailchimp and Squarespace, I led growth and mobile teams building marketing and content creation tools for small businesses and entrepreneurs. My teams shipped MarTech and content creation features, as well as running hundreds of experiments that drove product usage growth.
  • I led product strategy for Asana's core project & task management workflows, as well as an R&D-wide initiative to platformize the way engineers & designers build and reduce wasted code. During my time there, I worked with my fellow engineering, design, and data directors to improve culture for a staff of 40+ people.
  • As head of product at Supernormal, I shipped an AI-powered meeting notetaker and agentic assistant for knowledge workers.

I also advise early-stage startup founders on product development. Reach out if something piques your interest here.
